* **2.3 Formulasi Matematika Loss Function (Lengkap dengan Persamaan):**
* **Vanilla AE:** $\mathcal{L}_{AE} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \Vert{}x_i - D_\phi(E_\theta(x_i))\Vert{}_2^2$
* **Sparse AE:** $\mathcal{L}_{SAE} = \mathcal{L}_{AE} + \lambda \sum_{j} \vert{}h_j\vert{}$ (penalti regularisasi sparsity $L_1$ pada aktivasi laten)


* **VAE (ELBO):** $\mathcal{L}_{VAE} = \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi(z\vert{}x)}[\log p_\theta(x\vert{}z)] - D_{KL}(q_\phi(z\vert{}x) \,\Vert{}\, \mathcal{N}(0, I))$ + penjelasan *reparameterization trick* $z = \mu + \sigma \odot \epsilon$
* **Vanilla GAN & DCGAN (Minimax Game):** $\min_G \max_D V(D, G) = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{data}}[\log D(x)] + \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p_z}[\log(1 - D(G(z)))]$
* **DDPM (Noise Prediction Objective):** $\mathcal{L}_{simple} = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_0, \epsilon} \left[ \Vert{}\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} x_0 + \sqrt{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon, t)\Vert{}_2^2 \right]$
